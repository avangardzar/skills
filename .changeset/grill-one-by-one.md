Add the `grill-one-by-one` skill (engineering bucket, user-invoked). It is the same docs-writing grilling session as `grill-with-docs`, with the rounds swapped for exactly one question at a time: it asks a single question, holds it open while you interrogate it back, records the answer as a `✅` line, and only then asks the next. Writing stays with `domain-modeling`, so `CONTEXT.md` and the ADRs come out the same as before. Pick between the two by your own state, not by the topic: you know it, take rounds; you do not, take one at a time.
